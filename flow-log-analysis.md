## Security Findings

### 1. SSH Brute Force Attempt
- Source: 85.170.119.34
- Rejected attempts: 111 in a single 5-minute window
- Detection: CloudWatch alarm `rejected-ssh-attempts` (metric filter on dstPort=22, action=REJECT), threshold >100/5min
- Result: Alarm transitioned OK → ALARM → OK as the burst occurred and aged out
- Note: a second, much smaller source (37.55.118.42) also hit port 22 — likely opportunistic internet scanning, not part of the test

### 2. Port Scan Detected
- Source: 85.170.119.34
- Unique ports attempted: 27 (crossed the >20 threshold)
- Ports included: 21, 23, 25, 110, 143, 443, 445, 465, 587, 993, 995, 1433, 1521, 2049, 3306, 3389, 5432, 5900, 5985, 6379, 8080, 8443, 9200, 9300, 27017, plus 22 and 80
- Detection: Logs Insights query counting distinct dstPort per srcAddr, filtered >20
- Additional context: 87 total sources exceeded the 20-port threshold in the full dataset — most are background internet scanning traffic unrelated to this exercise, not generated by the lab
